About the clinic

Care from a doctor who explains, not just treats.

Clinique du Cèdre opened in Achrafieh in 2015 with one goal: give Beirut families dermatology care that's thorough, unhurried, and honest about cost from the first visit. Every consultation ends with a written plan — what we found, what we recommend, and what it costs — so you can decide with your family, not on the spot.
